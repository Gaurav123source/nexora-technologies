Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our website development services, process, technologies, and support. If you have any other queries, feel free to contact us.

What services does Nexora Technologies provide?

We provide professional website development, custom web application development, UI/UX design, database solutions, and ongoing maintenance support to help businesses build a strong digital presence.

How long does it take to develop a website?

The development time depends on the project requirements, design complexity, and features. A basic website usually takes 2–4 weeks, while custom web applications may require more time.

Do you create responsive websites for mobile devices?

Yes, we build fully responsive websites that work smoothly across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices to provide the best user experience.

What technologies do you use for development?

We use modern technologies including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, React.js, Node.js, Express.js, MySQL, and other tools based on project requirements.

Do you provide website maintenance and support after delivery?

Yes, we provide ongoing support and maintenance services to ensure your website remains secure, updated, and performs efficiently after launch.

How can I start my project with Nexora Technologies?

Simply contact us with your project idea and requirements. Our team will discuss your goals, suggest the right solution, and guide you through the development process.
